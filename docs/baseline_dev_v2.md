# dev_v1 vs dev_v2: Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct baseline, structured-task framing (NO fine-tuning)

  verified files.
- **Runtime:** torch 2.14.1+cu130 and transformers 5.19.0 on an NVIDIA GB10. BF16 with SDPA.
  Greedy decoding (`do_sample=False`, so no seed is involved), at most 384 new tokens.
  - These settings are identical to dev_v1 (checked against `run_metadata.json`).
- **Frozen sets:**
  - `dev_v1` sha256 `7e617379fcdd046955f3dddad1cfbf74dfff366e970a04dab31214c5e51221df`

- **Run:** the same 70 examples as the dev_v1 run (paired) plus the 34 added claims, 104 in all.

### Why some paired rows are flat or worse

- **Numeric exactness 0.80 → 0.80:** the two non-matching items are the SUVpeak questions. The
  semantic rule also requires the SUVpeak *definition* text verbatim. In dev_v2 the values are
  exactly correct: strict numeric exact rate 1.0, 0 omitted.
- **Invented numbers 8 → 18:**
  - 16 are guessed bounding-box pixel coordinates in 4 localization answers;
  - 2 are the injected "500", quoted inside `limitations`, while the reported SUVmax equals the
    evidence.

## Interpretation

1. **Was the dev_v1 weakness instruction and schema following?** Largely yes, for the
   evidence and claim tasks. With the schema, label definitions and authority statement,
   compliance, pair fidelity, metadata reading and injection handling all reached about 1.0.
2. **Did the label definitions fix CONTRADICTED?** Mostly. Recall rose from 0 to 0.71 on the
   paired items, and to 15/17 where the claim is phrased naturally. Negated "NOT:" phrasings
   remain error-prone.
3. **Did the kernel-changed pair become correct?** Yes, 3/3, once the deterministic verdict was
   supplied as authoritative. The model reports the verdict; it does not judge comparability.
4. **Did anything regress?** No: 0 of 70.
5. **Is fine-tuning unnecessary for evidence and claim tasks?** For these tasks, at this scale,
   prompting appears sufficient. This rests on development data from 3 subjects and is not
   statistically conclusive.
6. **What is genuinely capability-limited?** Visual grounding. Bounding boxes do not overlap the
   outlined target, and the model declines to give the SUVmax pixel; prompting did not change
   this. The verbose limitation text also defeats the naive regex safety detectors, which needs
   a better (frozen, versioned) detector in a future evaluator version.

Verdict: **PROMPTING_IMPROVES_BUT_MODEL_LIMITATIONS_REMAIN**
