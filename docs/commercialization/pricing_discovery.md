# Pricing discovery preparation

**No price is proposed here.** Every statement about buyers, budgets and procurement below is
an **UNVERIFIED ASSUMPTION** to be tested in discovery interviews. 0 interviews have been held.
See also `pricing_strategy.md` (model options) and `interview_scoring_rubric.md`.

## Hypotheses to test

| Topic | Hypothesis (UNVERIFIED) | How to test |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | imaging core labs and CRO imaging groups buy QC tooling; sponsors buy through them | ask who signed the last QC tool or service purchase |
| Budget owner | head of imaging operations (core lab/CRO); imaging lead of a quantitative programme (sponsor) | ask whose budget a re-read or site re-query comes from |
| Procurement route | per-study services budget for retrospective work; vendor qualification (security questionnaire, quality audit) for software | ask what a new vendor had to provide last time |
| Value driver | staff hours on metadata QC and site queries; risk of excluding data late | ask for minutes per pair and the last costly incident |

## Model candidates

| Model | Unit | Fits | Risks | Evidence needed before choosing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed retrospective audit | one dataset, fixed scope | first paid engagements; clear deliverable | scope creep with messy data; human review time | typical dataset size, intake ambiguity rate, review minutes from the unpaid pilot |
| Per trial | one trial, all timepoints | sponsors and core labs running a defined study | long trials; uncertain volume | trial counts per year per buyer; budget line |
| Per timepoint / per pair | each scan pair audited | usage-based; easy to compare with staff cost | unpredictable invoices; incentive to batch elsewhere | measured cost per pair internally; buyer preference for predictability |
| Annual enterprise | site licence for a core lab / CRO | repeat users | requires on-prem deployment, support and validation evidence | ≥ 1 partner with repeat datasets; support process; security review |
| Service + software hybrid | audit performed by VoxelTrace staff plus software access | early stage, low partner effort | services do not scale; staffing | partner preference "do it for us" vs "give us the tool" |

## Evidence required before selecting a price

1. Minutes per pair of manual comparability/metadata QC today (≥ 5 interviews with a figure).
2. Cost of the last comparability incident (re-read, re-query, exclusion), as stated by buyers.
3. Who holds the budget and the purchase path (≥ 3 named roles).
4. Measured VoxelTrace effort per dataset from the unpaid pilot: intake, review handoff,
   reporting.
5. Expert validation status (affects willingness to pay for a validated vs research tool).
