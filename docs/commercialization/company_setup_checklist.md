# Company / operating checklist

This is a planning checklist only. It is **not legal, tax, financial or insurance advice**,
and completing an item does not mean it is legally sufficient. Each item needs the
appropriate qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

| # | Item | Questions to resolve | Who to ask | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corporate entity | jurisdiction; entity type; founder equity and vesting; where IP will sit | corporate lawyer, accountant | not started |
| 2 | Business bank and accounting | separate account; bookkeeping; invoicing; tax registration (incl. VAT/GST/sales tax where relevant) | accountant | not started |
| 3 | Domain and email | product domain; professional email; DNS security (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) | — | not started |
| 4 | Contracts | NDA template; data-use agreement; pilot/statement-of-work; services agreement; software licence; order form | commercial lawyer | not started |
| 5 | Privacy policy | website and contact-form data; any personal data handled during pilots | privacy counsel | not started |
| 6 | Terms | website terms; software licence terms for non-open components; acceptable use | commercial lawyer | not started |
| 7 | Professional liability (discussion) | exposure from audit findings relied on by customers; limitation-of-liability wording | broker, lawyer | not started |
| 8 | Cyber insurance (discussion) | cover for incidents involving customer data, even if pilots run locally | broker | not started |
| 9 | Data processing agreements | controller/processor roles per jurisdiction; sub-processors (none planned for local runs) | privacy counsel | not started |
| 10 | IP ownership | assignment of all existing code, docs and rules from founder(s) to the company; employer and university IP policies for anyone who contributed | IP lawyer | not started |
| 11 | Contributor agreements | CLA or DCO for outside contributions to the open repository | IP lawyer | not started |
| 12 | Open-source dependencies | licence inventory exists (`../licence_attribution_audit.md`; no copyleft found); a policy for adding new dependencies; notices when redistributing | IP lawyer (review) | inventory done; policy not started |
| 13 | Trademark / name search | "VoxelTrace" availability in target classes and countries; domain and social handles | trademark attorney | not started |
| 14 | Regulatory counsel | confirm the intended-use boundary (`intended_use_draft.md`); trial-software expectations from sponsors | regulatory consultant | not started |
| 15 | Quality-management roadmap | when to start a QMS; scope (`quality_system_roadmap.md`) | QA/RA consultant | roadmap drafted |
| 16 | Data citation and attribution in deliverables | CC BY obligations for public data used in demos | — | identified (`../licence_attribution_audit.md`) |
