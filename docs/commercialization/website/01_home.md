# Website page: Home

**Headline:** Quantitative trust for PET imaging.

**Subhead:** Know whether your PET scans are comparable before anyone reads an SUV change.

## Sections

- The problem: timepoints from different scanners, software versions, reconstructions and uptake times can make SUV changes meaningless, and this is often found late.
- What VoxelTrace does: audits every baseline/follow-up pair from the DICOM headers against QIBA FDG 1.14, EANM FDG 2.0 and PERCIST 1.0 and explains each result.
- What you get: a one-page summary, a pair table, site/scanner summary, draft site queries and a checksummed evidence package.
- How it runs: locally, offline, deterministic.

**CTA:** Request a retrospective pilot

**Validation note (shown on the page):** Research and trial-QC software, not for clinical use. External expert validation is pending. Siemens scanners validated on real public longitudinal pairs; GE ingestion only; Philips metadata only.

## Claims allowed now

- audits PET comparability across timepoints, sites, scanners and protocols
- every verdict traceable to a DICOM field, value and source
- missing evidence is reported, never treated as a pass
- runs locally; no AI in the audit

## Claims NOT allowed now

- clinically validated
- FDA / Health Canada / CE / EU MDR approved, cleared or compliant
- diagnoses, stages or classifies treatment response
- validated for all vendors
- guarantees comparability
- guarantees cost savings or regulator acceptance
- AI-powered / uses AI to decide
