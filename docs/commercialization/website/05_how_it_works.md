## Claims allowed now

- no step uses AI
- nothing is guessed; missing values stay missing

## Claims NOT allowed now

- clinically validated
- FDA / Health Canada / CE / EU MDR approved, cleared or compliant
- diagnoses, stages or classifies treatment response
- validated for all vendors
- guarantees comparability
- guarantees cost savings or regulator acceptance
- AI-powered / uses AI to decide
