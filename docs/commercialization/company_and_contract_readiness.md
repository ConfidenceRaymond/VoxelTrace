# Company and contract readiness checklist

**USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED.** This is a checklist of decisions and documents, not
legal advice and not a completed legal package. Nothing here has been drafted, reviewed or
signed. Jurisdiction-specific requirements must come from qualified counsel.

| # | Item | Why it matters for a pilot | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Company entity | contracts, invoicing and liability need a legal party | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED: choose jurisdiction and form |
| 2 | Banking / accounting | receive payment, invoice, track costs | NOT STARTED | USER ACTION REQUIRED |
| 3 | Domain and e-mail | professional contact; the starter kit and website need a real address | NOT STARTED | USER ACTION REQUIRED (no address is invented in any document) |
| 4 | NDA (mutual) | discuss partner data needs and roadmap | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED |
| 5 | DUA (data-use agreement) | lawful receipt and use of de-identified data; defines purpose, retention, deletion | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED; partner institutions usually supply their own template |
| 6 | DPA (data-processing agreement) | where personal data protection law applies (e.g. pseudonymised data under GDPR) | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED: determine whether VoxelTrace is processor, controller or neither for each pilot |
| 7 | MSA (master services agreement) | terms for repeat work | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED (only after a first pilot) |
| 8 | SOW / pilot agreement | scope, deliverables, timeline, fees (if any), acceptance | TEMPLATE ONLY | `../pilot/external_partner/PILOT_SCOPE_TEMPLATE.md` is a scope template, not a contract |
| 9 | Liability discussion | research-use outputs; no clinical use; limits of liability | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED |

| 11 | IP ownership | who owns code, outputs, improvements; partner data stays the partner's | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED (`ip_and_moat.md` has the technical inventory) |

| 13 | Open-source dependency review | licences of dependencies and public data | DONE (technical) | `../licence_attribution_audit.md`; counsel to confirm for commercial distribution |

| 15 | Privacy policy | website and any contact form | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED |
| 16 | Terms of use | website, downloadable starter kit | NOT STARTED | USER / LEGAL COUNSEL ACTION REQUIRED |
| 17 | Incident process | data or result incidents during a pilot | DRAFT (operational) | `../pilot/first_real_pilot_operations.md` §incident; counsel to review notification duties |

and #17 reviewed. Minimum before accepting payment: additionally #1, #2, #8, #9, #10 (if required
