# Design-partner questions

1. How do you currently decide whether a baseline/follow-up PET pair is quantitatively comparable? Who decides, and how long does it take per pair?
2. Which standard(s) does your charter reference (QIBA, EANM/EARL, PERCIST, sponsor-specific)?
3. What fraction of pairs are excluded or queried today, and for which reasons?
4. Do sites send reconstruction parameters outside DICOM (forms, exports)? In what format?
5. Which scanners and vendors are in your trials? Any long-axial-FOV or PET/MR?
6. What de-identification profile do you apply? Are height, weight, sex and timing retained?
7. What output would your QC team or sponsor accept: CSV, PDF, bundle, integration into an existing system?
8. Who would review reference regions and adjudicate disputed pairs?
9. What would make you trust an automated "cannot decide": reason codes, evidence, an expert spot-check?
10. What data could you share for a retrospective pilot, and under what agreement?
