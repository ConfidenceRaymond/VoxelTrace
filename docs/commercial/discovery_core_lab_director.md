# Discovery interview: imaging core-lab director (20 min)

**Rules:** listen. Do not pitch, do not describe software features, and do not claim results,
pricing or regulatory status. Record no patient information.

1. **Current comparability workflow.** How does your lab decide that a PET timepoint pair is
   usable for the primary endpoint? Is it written in the imaging charter? Who performs it, and
   who signs it?
2. **Reconstruction metadata.** At site qualification, how do you capture reconstruction
   settings? How often do trial exports differ from what was qualified? How do you find out?
3. **Undecidable cases.** What share of pairs is queried, excluded or accepted with a
   deviation? Is "insufficient information" tracked as its own category?
4. **Time spent.** Analyst hours per pair and per trial on comparability QC. How does that
   scale with the number of sites and scanners?
5. **Failure incidents.** Describe the last protocol deviation found late (for example after
   database lock or in an audit). What was the root cause, and what did it cost?
6. **Site queries.** How are queries raised, tracked and closed? Typical turnaround? What makes
   a query easy for a site to answer?
7. **Build vs buy.** What do you use today: in-house tools, vendor software, spreadsheets? What
   did you last buy for QC, and why?
8. **Budget ownership.** Is QC priced per timepoint, per study, or absorbed? Who would own a new
   QC line item, the lab or the sponsor?

**Close:** would a retrospective audit of a closed trial be useful to you, and under what
agreement could de-identified data be examined?
