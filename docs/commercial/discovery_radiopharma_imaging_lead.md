# Discovery interview: radiopharmaceutical sponsor imaging lead (20 min)

**Rules:** listen. Do not pitch, do not describe software features, and do not claim results,
pricing or regulatory status. Record no patient information.

1. **Current comparability workflow.** In your current programmes, who decides that
   quantitative PET timepoints are comparable: the core lab, the sites, your team? What
   evidence reaches you?
2. **Reconstruction metadata.** Do you require harmonised reconstruction (for example EARL)?
   How do you verify it was used at every timepoint and site?
3. **Undecidable cases.** How are pairs reported when comparability cannot be shown? Does that
   affect the endpoint analysis or regulatory submissions?
4. **Time spent.** How much programme time goes to imaging QC queries and reconciliation before
   an interim or final analysis?
5. **Failure incidents.** Has a quantitative imaging endpoint been questioned (internally, by a
   DSMB or by a regulator) because of acquisition or reconstruction inconsistency? What
   happened?
6. **Site queries.** How visible are site queries to you? Which recur across trials?
7. **Build vs buy.** Do you rely entirely on the core lab, or keep internal checks? Would you
   want an independent check, and at which stage?
8. **Budget ownership.** Which budget pays for imaging QC: clinical operations, imaging, or
   outsourced? Who would decide on an additional audit?

**Close:** is there a completed study where a retrospective comparability audit would answer a
question you still have? What agreement would that need?
