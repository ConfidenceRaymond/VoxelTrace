# Discovery interview: PET physicist (20 min)

**Rules:** listen. Do not pitch, do not describe software features, and do not claim results,
pricing or regulatory status. Record answers in the interviewee's own words. Record no patient
information.

1. **Current comparability workflow.** Take me through the last baseline/follow-up pair you
   checked before quantitative analysis. What did you open first? What did you check, and in
   which order?
2. **Reconstruction metadata.** Where do you find the reconstruction settings: DICOM header,
   private tags, a site form, the scanner console? How often are they missing or only free
   text? What do you do then?
3. **Undecidable cases.** When you cannot decide whether a pair is comparable, what happens?
   Is "cannot decide" a recorded outcome, or does it become include or exclude?
4. **Time spent.** Minutes per pair on a typical day and on a bad day. Which step takes
   longest?
5. **Failure incidents.** Tell me about a time a non-comparable pair reached an analysis. How
   was it found, and what did it cost?
6. **Site queries.** What do you ask sites for most often? How long do answers take? How often
   is the answer "we don't know"?
7. **Build vs buy.** Have you built scripts or checklists for this? Who maintains them? What
   would make you stop using them?
8. **Budget ownership.** If this check were done differently, whose budget would it come from?
   Who signs off?

**Close:** who else should we talk to? Could a completed trial be examined retrospectively
under a data-use agreement?
