# Customer discovery script (30 min)

**Rule:** listen; do not pitch; do not claim results, pricing or regulatory status.

1. **Context (5 min).** Your role, trials, imaging core lab involvement, PET volume.
2. **Current process (10 min).** Walk through the last time a PET pair could not be used.
   - What was missing?
   - How was it found?
   - How long did resolution take?
   - Who paid the cost?
3. **Evidence and trust (5 min).** What evidence do you need to accept or reject a pair? How
   are site queries handled?
4. **Data (5 min).** Formats, de-identification, private tags, reconstruction records,
   scanner mix.
5. **Close (5 min).** Would a retrospective audit of a completed trial be useful? Who else
   should we talk to? Could data be shared under a data-use agreement?

**Record:** pains in their words, frequency, current workaround, decision maker.
**Do not record:** identifiable patient information.
